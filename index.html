Privacy Policy

Long Exposure Calculator (the "App") — com.longexposurecalc.app
Last updated: 2026-11-02 | Version: V1.0
In one sentence: this App collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. Every calculation runs on your device. The only network traffic is Google Play billing, and only when you buy or restore Pro.

This is not "not implemented yet". It is the product: the App is free to download and earns money only from an optional one-time Pro purchase. Revenue does not depend on monetising data, so there is nothing to collect.

2. Your numbers stay on your device

  • All math is local. ND stops, stacked filters, corrected shutter times, reciprocity data and the countdown are computed by code running on your phone. No server calculates anything for you, and no result is sent anywhere.
  • Saved data. Language, units, saved presets, the last film stock you picked and the Pro entitlement cache are written as plain JSON files inside the App's private directory. They are removed when you uninstall the App.
  • The timer. It stores only the moment the exposure ends, and hands that absolute time to Android's AlarmManager so the phone can wake up even in Doze. The notification it shows is generated on the device; the label it contains is the one you typed. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

3. The only network traffic: Google Play billing

The App makes no HTTP requests of its own — no weather, no sync, no cloud, no update check. Network access exists only because Google Play's billing library needs it when you tap Buy or Restore:

Who talks to the networkWhat happens
Google Play Billing (library code) Talks to Google Play to start or restore the purchase. The App receives back only whether the purchase is active, plus a purchase token.
The App's own Dart code No requests. There is no HTTP client in the application code.

The billing library bundles Google's datatransport component, which is why INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE appear in the merged manifest. The App never initialises that component, so there is no telemetry path in practice — but the permission is genuinely present in the package, which is why it is listed below instead of being hidden.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children and collects no one's personal information (see §1), so no special handling of children's data applies.

6. Data deletion

Because the App collects and stores nothing on any server, there is no "your data" to delete on our side.

  • Everything the App saves (settings, presets, entitlement cache) lives in its private directory and is fully removed when you uninstall.
  • To clear it without uninstalling: Android Settings → Apps → Long Exposure Calculator → Storage → Clear data.
  • A purchase itself is tied to your Google account, not to this device. Deleting app data does not delete the purchase; use Restore in the App's settings to bring it back.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version ever adds collection behaviour (for example cloud sync, which would necessarily transfer your data), this policy will be updated before the change takes effect and highlighted in-app.

The App's existing commitments do not change: the core calculator (exposure conversion, ND stops, stacked filters, the countdown with lock-screen alarms) is free, with no watermark, no time limit, and no ads.
